\caption{\textbf{Frozen-topology ablation (LLM-9B, social-bias coupling).}
Three \emph{imposed} bond graphs, run with the social-bias machinery frozen:
\emph{No-bonds} (empty graph), \emph{Allied-pair} (one agent pair allied),
and \emph{Allied-all} (fully connected). Mean~$\pm$~std over pooled episodes
($n{=}6$; 2 seeds $\times$ 3 episodes). \emph{Coop.\ milest.}\ = distinct
team milestones in Ch2--Ch5; \emph{All milest.}\ = team total including Ch1
and the communication track; \emph{Task return} = team-summed undiffused
reward. \emph{Credit Gini} measures inequality of per-agent milestone credit
($0$ = equal, $1$ = one agent earns all); \emph{Excl.\ share} = fraction of
milestone credit earned by agent~2 (the excluded agent under
\emph{Allied-pair}; uniform $\approx 0.33$). Best in each $\uparrow$ column in
\textbf{bold}; the maximal-coupling arm is shaded.}
