\caption{\textbf{Episode-horizon ablation of the frozen topologies (LLM-9B,
social-bias coupling).} The three imposed graphs re-run at $1{,}000$ vs.\
$2{,}000$ steps. To isolate the horizon from sampling, both budgets use the
\emph{same} two seeds (42, 123; $n{=}6$, 3 episodes each); the full six-seed
$1{,}000$-step figures are in \autoref{tab:conditions_results}.
\emph{Coop.\ milest.}\ = distinct Ch2--Ch5 team milestones; \emph{All
milest.}\ = team total incl.\ Ch1 and the comm.\ track; \emph{Task return} =
team-summed undiffused reward; \emph{Credit Gini} = inequality of per-agent
milestone credit ($0$ = equal); \emph{Excl.\ share} = share earned by
agent~2 (excluded under \emph{Allied-pair}; uniform $\approx 0.33$).
\emph{Eps.\ completing} counts the six episodes reaching each load-bearing
milestone: M8 (first Ch2 anvil), M13 (Ch3 switch), M15 (Ch3 regroup --- all
three in the communal room), M17 (first Ch4 mob kill); the Ch4 arena clear
(M18) and Ch5 boss (M23) are never reached ($0/6$ throughout, omitted).
Doubling the horizon lifts every condition on every performance metric, but
the gain is individually-reachable progression --- M13 saturates to $6/6$
and M17 rises to $2$--$3/6$ --- while the milestones that demand tight
coordination, the Ch2 anvil (M8) and the Ch3 regroup (M15), stay at
${\leq}1$ episode regardless of budget: no topology separates from the null
at either horizon and the imposed exclusion is never realised.}
