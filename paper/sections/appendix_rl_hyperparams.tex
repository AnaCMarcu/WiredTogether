\section{Reinforcement-learning hyperparameters}
\label{sec:rl_hyperparams}

\autoref{tab:rl_hyperparams} lists the settings used for the MAPPO layer described in \autoref{sec:rl_formulation}. Reward normalisation is applied only in the IPPO ablation: under the centralised critic both the per-agent and team rewards are kept on the raw milestone scale, so that the GAE deltas $r'_{i,t}+\gamma V_\psi(s^{t+1})-V_\psi(s^t)$ are computed on a single consistent scale.
