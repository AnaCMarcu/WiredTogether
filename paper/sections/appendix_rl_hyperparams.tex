\subsection{Reinforcement-learning hyperparameters}
\label{sec:rl_hyperparams}

Reward normalisation is applied only in the IPPO ablation: under the centralised critic both the per-agent and team rewards are kept on the raw milestone scale, so that the GAE deltas $r'_{i,t}+\gamma V_\psi(s^{t+1})-V_\psi(s^t)$ are computed on a single consistent scale.

\subsection{Hebbian social-plasticity hyperparameters}
\label{sec:hebbian_hyperparams}

\autoref{tab:hebbian_hyperparams} lists the hyperparameters of the reward-modulated Hebbian social module (\autoref{sec:hebbian_method}). Unlike the RL settings above, none of these are learned: the graph $\mathbf{W}$ carries no gradient, and every value below is a fixed constant of the local update rule. The symbols match the co-activity signal (Eqs.~\eqref{eq:engagement}--\eqref{eq:coactivity}), the two-term bond update (Eq.~\eqref{eq:hebbian_update}), and reward diffusion (Eq.~\eqref{eq:diffusion}). The reward scale $r_{\max}$ is not a tuned constant but a running maximum of observed absolute rewards, kept here for completeness.
