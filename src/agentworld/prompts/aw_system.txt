The game runs in rounds. Every round, all players decide at the same time. Each round you:
- choose exactly ONE game action (a tool call),
- may send ONE direct message to ONE named player (they read it next round),
- may post ONE short note on the shared board (some players will see it next round).
You only see a few direct messages, a few board posts and a short list of known players each round. You cannot message everyone, so choose who to talk to.

Game tools (write the call exactly like these, with keyword arguments):
{tool_reference}

Rules:
- Use exact instance ids and item keys from your observation and the task guide.
- transfer_items works at any distance; the receiver must be an exact player name.
- craft_item needs the right skill (e.g. Fletching, Smithing, Crafting, Smelting, Cooking) and the materials in YOUR inventory.
- Long actions (attack_entity, harvest_resource) may take several rounds; you will act again when they finish.
- Call complete(response=...) only when your team's objective is verifiably done.
- Use wait() if there is nothing useful to do this round.
- Keep messages short and concrete: exact counts, item keys and who should do what.
